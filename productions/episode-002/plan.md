## Why these rounds

Uzbek YouTube research for this series found that locally debatable everyday choices outperform generic quizzes. The strongest published example is Tez Top!’s «Qaysi birini tanlaysiz? - Qora Gentra YOKI Oq Cobalt» (about 908 thousand views). This episode leads with that same pair, then interleaves other car, food, phone, city, wedding, and seasonal choices so two car rounds are not back to back.

Rounds, in order:

1. Qora Gentra YOKI oq Cobalt
2. Manti YOKI chuchvara
3. iPhone YOKI Samsung
4. Matiz YOKI Spark
5. Toshkent YOKI viloyat
6. Katta toʻy YOKI kichik toʻy
7. Yozda Chorvoqda YOKI qishda Chimyonda

None of the pilot rounds is reused (palov/somsa, choy/qatiq, Samarqand/Buxoro, doira/rubob, kurash/futbol, sumalak/holva, bozor/hovli).

Brand names are spoken because the choice is the point. Still images must hide badges, logos, model lettering, and readable plates. No claim about quality, price, engines, cameras, or market share is spoken.

## Facts marked in the script

Spoken claims, and where they were checked. The Tekshiruv section of `script.md` holds the same notes and the source URLs. Nothing in that section is read aloud.

1. Gentra and Cobalt. Both were produced at the Asaka plant (then GM Uzbekistan). Serial production of Cobalt started in September 2012; Gentra (Lacetti II) started in November 2013. The script speaks only the years. No engine, price, or quality comparison.
2. Manti and chuchvara. Manti are large and steamed in a qasqon. Chuchvara are small, boiled, and often served in broth or with qatiq. A folk claim that a good cook fits a dozen on one spoon is not spoken.
3. iPhone and Samsung. The first iPhone went on sale on 29 June 2007. Samsung presented the first Galaxy S on 23 March 2010. The script speaks only the years, and says «sotuvga chiqqan» for the iPhone and «taqdim etilgan» for the Galaxy S. No price, camera, or market-share comparison.
4. Matiz and Spark. Matiz production at Asaka started in August 2001; Spark production started in August 2010. The script speaks only those start years. The later end of Matiz production is not spoken.
5. Toshkent and the region. Tashkent became the capital on 17 August 1930, when the capital moved from Samarkand. As of 1 January 2026, official permanent population is 3,178,100 in Tashkent city and 3,160,700 in Tashkent Region — both over three million. The script does not speak the exact thousands.
6. Weddings. The national ICH entry «Nikoh» describes a wedding as several ceremonies. One early stage is fotiha toʻyi, also called kichik toʻy in some places; the main wedding is katta toʻy. No guest-count is spoken, because a stable official average was not found.
7. Chorvoq and Chimyon. meros.uz gives the Chorvoq dam height as 168 m and the reservoir’s date as 1970 (built 1963–1970). Uzbekistan Travel gives Greater Chimyon as 3,309 m. The script speaks the dam height, the year 1970, and the Greater Chimyon height.
